# AURELION Wiki
## Welcome to the eagle's world

AURELION is a floating golden eagle companion for Android. It is designed to inhabit the edges of your screen with small moments of movement, curiosity, calm, and playful interaction.

### Status labels
**Implemented foundation** means the repository contains a basic implementation; it does not guarantee production-ready behavior on every device. **Planned** means not implemented. **Experimental** means a concept that may change or be dropped.

### The north star
AURELION should be a companion that feels alive without demanding attention. It must be easy to pause, hide, mute, or ignore. It should never punish the user for being away.
