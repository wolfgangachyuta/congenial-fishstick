# Feature Laboratory — Wild Ideas
Ideas below are experiments, not commitments. Prioritize delight, feasibility, privacy, and battery cost.

## Near-term: big delight, small engineering
- **Mood engine:** behavior weights change with a simple mood model.
- **Tap acknowledgements:** looks toward the touch, blinks, tilts, or chirps.
- **Micro-motion:** feather shake, stretch, foot shuffle, head tilt, wing tuck.
- **Favorite perch:** a chosen spot it occasionally returns to.
- **Time-of-day cadence:** brighter in the morning, calmer late at night, entirely local.
- **Focus perch:** quiet companion during a user-started timer, then a tiny celebration.
- **Rare flight:** a manually triggered or infrequent short flight flourish.
- **Pose photo mode:** user selects a pose and captures a shareable image.

## Mid-term: make it feel like a tiny world
- **Ambient seasons:** cosmetic, optional seasonal details.
- **Weather mood:** manual or opt-in data; rain may make it tuck its wings, breeze may prompt feather ruffling.
- **Mini habitat:** a perch, nest, branch, or rock that can be customized.
- **Feather collection:** earn cosmetic colors and accessories through play, never pressure.
- **Discovery moments:** rare animation combinations that are not tied to addictive reward schedules.
- **Soundscape:** optional chirps, landing sounds, and wing rustles with volume controls.

## Long-term: delightful and strange
- **Screen-edge climbing:** eagle explores screen edges with a safe interaction zone.
- **Shadow awareness:** its own shadow shifts subtly during movement.
- **Tiny astronomy:** night sky themed perch or star-gazing pose (no location needed).
- **Museum mode:** eagle becomes still while the user reads or views photos; only if user explicitly enables context sensing.
- **Companion postcard:** create a snapshot with a decorative frame and selected pose.
- **Behavior scripting:** users build simple rules such as "after focus timer, stretch" without code.
- **Community behavior packs:** installable animation/behavior sets with permissions and trust boundaries.
- **AR perch:** experimental camera/AR placement, strictly opt-in and foreground-only.
- **Creature journal:** optional local log of favorite poses or milestones, easy to erase.
- **Silent companionship:** a mode that removes dialogue and sound entirely but preserves visual presence.

## Hard constraints
- No constant screen scraping or invasive monitoring.
- No always-on microphone by default.
- No notifications designed to guilt the user.
- No hidden cloud processing.
- No behavior that obstructs important UI or accessibility.
- Battery and privacy budgets are product requirements, not later polish.
